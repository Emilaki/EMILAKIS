How this pilot protects members

  • Tasks are free to access; no pay-to-unlock reward offers.
  • Fixed reward shown before a task; reward is paid only from reserved sponsor funds.
  • Withdrawal request minimum KSh 50 and maximum KSh 500 per rolling 24-hour period.
  • All withdrawals require operator review. Daraja sandbox requests are simulations and do not send real money.
  • Live payouts are disabled. Never share your M-Pesa PIN or one-time password.
